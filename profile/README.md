We build VR platforms that let you train decisions, teamwork and life-saving action again and again — in realistic scenarios, without risk, and without compromising clinical accuracy.

## What we build

- **Disaster & Emergency Response Training** — a multi-user VR simulation where fire services, police, authorities and rescue services train together in the same scenario, each from their own perspective. A gamemaster controls the scenario in real time, just like in a real crisis.
- **First Aid & CPR Training** — VR training in first aid and CPR built on recognised clinical guidelines, covering chest compressions, AED use, and the critical first minutes of a response.
- **Academy** — training for gamemasters and CPR instructors who lead the sessions above.
- **Labs** — our internal R&D environment for new VR concepts, scenarios and technologies, developed with clinicians, educators and gamemasters.
